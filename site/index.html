Powered by Hister, SearXNG and Obsidian, Machiya's apps make it easy to find and work with everything you've come across on the Information Superhighway.

Install Source

Search your pages

Hister and Shiori

Browse as usual: Hister's official extensions save what you read in Firefox and Chrome, and hister-safari does it in Safari on the Mac. Shiori also comes with its own Safari extension for your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Search the World Wide Web

SearXNG

Search results from SearXNG and Hister appear side by side: the web next to the pages you've read. The search engines see your query, never your history.

Search your code

Forgejo and GitHub

Connect Forgejo or GitHub. READMEs, issues, pull requests and releases from your repositories get a Code tab of their own.

Read, curate and publish your notes

Kura and Niwa

Point Kura at your Obsidian vaults and your notes show up next to your pages, code and search results. Publish the ones you want to share to a digital garden that grows with your projects.

It's the little things

  • Native share sheet support on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • Offline support. Saved pages sync when you're back on The Internet.
  • Notes open in Obsidian, or preview in Machiya's rooms.
  • Archive.org and Archive.is support: every result links to its snapshots, and Niwa adds an archive.org link beside every link it publishes.
  • Meet your audience where they are: Niwa publishes to the Web, Gopher and Gemini.
  • A fully featured kanban board, and a writing kit that lays out a finished project for its blog post.
  • Keep your AI agents in the loop with skills and a fully featured MCP server.
  • Runs in containers or natively on Linux, FreeBSD, NetBSD and OpenBSD.
  • Multi-architecture support for amd64 and arm64.
  • Native Shiori apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Linux and Haiku, and progressive web apps for everything else. No Electron.
  • Support for your esoteric tastes: native apps for Haiku, Classic Macintosh (coming soon) and others.
