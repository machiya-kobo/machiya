Washi grades for lantern skins

Washi is graded by weight in grams per square metre. For a folding chochin skin, 25 to 35 gsm keeps the light warm and the folds crisp.

Kozo fibre washi is the strongest; mitsumata is smoother and takes ink well. Gampi is translucent but tears on tight ribs.

Test a scrap first: fold it twenty times along one line and hold it to a candle.
