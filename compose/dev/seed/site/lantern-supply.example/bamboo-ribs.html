Pre-split bamboo ribs

Our bamboo ribs are split from three-year madake culms, 2 mm wide and 0.8 mm thick, soaked and ready to wind.

A spiral rib wound on a form gives a lantern that collapses like a spring. Fourteen turns suit a 30 cm lantern.
