# Glue and paste

Wheat-starch paste (**nori**) is reversible, food-safe and dries flat. PVA is faster but yellows and cannot be undone. For anything meant to last, use nori.

Recipe: 1 part starch to 8 parts water, stirred over low heat until clear. Cool before use. Tested for the [[Lantern]] skin; see the [[Glue comparison]] card.
