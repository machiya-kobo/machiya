# Bamboo frames

A folding frame is **one continuous spiral rib** wound on a form, so it collapses like a spring.

- Split the culm into ribs about 2 mm wide; thinner than 1.5 mm and they splinter.
- Soak 20 minutes, then wind on the form and leave overnight.
- Sand the inside face smooth: rough ribs tear the [[Washi paper]].

```text
rib width     2.0 mm
rib thickness 0.8 mm
turns         14
```

Where the bamboo comes from: [[Sourcing bamboo]]. A jig for even ribs is on the board: [[Bamboo frame jig]].
