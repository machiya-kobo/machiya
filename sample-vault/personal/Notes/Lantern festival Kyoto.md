# Lantern festival Kyoto

Kyoto's autumn evenings are lit by hundreds of lanterns along temple paths. Stay in a [[Kyoto machiya townhouse]] if you can: the street front, the small garden and the storehouse at the back are the whole idea of this stack.

> [!info] Planning
> Dates change every year. The trip is a card on the board: [[Kyoto trip]]. Packing list: [[Kyoto trip packing]].
