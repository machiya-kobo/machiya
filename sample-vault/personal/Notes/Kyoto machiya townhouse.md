# Kyoto machiya townhouse

A *machiya* (町家) is a long, narrow townhouse: a **shop** (みせ) at the street front, a small inner **garden** (庭), and a **storehouse** (蔵) at the back, all under one roof.

Why it matters here: the same layout names the tools that keep this vault: a reader for the storehouse, a garden for what is published, a shop front for the board.
