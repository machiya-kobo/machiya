# Washi paper

Washi is paper made from the inner bark of **kozo** (mulberry), mitsumata or gampi. The long fibres make it strong when wet and translucent when lit, which is why it is the skin of every [[Paper lanterns|paper lantern]].

## Weights I use

| Weight | Use |
|---|---|
| 12 g/m² | tissue layers, patches |
| 25 g/m² | lantern skin |
| 40 g/m² | caps and reinforcement |

## Handling

1. Keep it flat and dry until the paste is ready.
2. Tear along a wet line instead of cutting: the feathered edge hides seams.
3. Let it dry under tension, never in the sun.

Paste: [[Glue and paste]]. Used in the [[Lantern]] project.
